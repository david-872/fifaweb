Welcome to your FIFA ticketing account!

The FIFA Resale Marketplace reopened on 2 April 2026 at 11:00 am (Eastern Time) / 17:00 (Central European Time).

The actions you can take within the Resale Marketplace include:

1. Reselling tickets

2. Withdrawing tickets listed for resale

3. Transferring tickets using the Ticket Transfer feature

4. Purchasing tickets listed for resale

If you are a Participating Member Association (PMA) Supporter, you may be eligible to purchase and resell Supporter Tickets from the Resale Marketplace. Each Participating Member Association sets its own criteria for whether its supporters can resell Supporter Tickets in the FIFA Resale Marketplace. If you choose to resell your PMA Supporter tickets, they will be available only to eligible fans, meaning your PMA Supporter ticket will be visible only to a specific number of fans.

Ticket resale prices on the FIFA Resale Marketplace may vary depending on the host country and ticket product. Please note that the fee for purchasing or reselling tickets on the FIFA Resale Marketplace is 15% of the total price, inclusive of taxes. The final cost, including this fee, will be displayed on the FIFA Resale Marketplace when purchasing or reselling tickets.

For more information on the FIFA Resale Marketplace, please visit our FAQs.
